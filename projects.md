# Projects

## Pusher Bot

**College of Design and Engineering · Jan 2025 – Apr 2025**

Built a custom MuJoCo–Gym simulation framework for contact-rich robotic pushing. Designed a structured observation space, multi-phase reward shaping, and proximity-based action scaling for 7-DOF manipulation. Trained an actor–critic PPO policy with GAE and achieved a 92.8% success rate over 1,000 evaluation episodes.

## Turtle Soft Robot

**College of Design and Engineering · Aug 2024 – Dec 2024**

Designed and built a bio-inspired soft robotic sea turtle using silicone bellow actuators and an ESP32-based pump–valve system. Added wireless multimodal control through PS4 Bluetooth inputs and Python speech-to-text commands.

## PillBuddy

**Medical Grand Challenge · Mar 2024 – Aug 2024**

Worked in a six-person interdisciplinary team to design and prototype an automated pill dispenser. Built the prototype using Arduino, 3D printing, and machining; the team reached the finals in the Nascent Category of Medical Grand Challenge 2024.
