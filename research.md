# Research

My research focuses on learning and control for versatile robotic systems, with an emphasis on transferring policies from simulation to real hardware.

## Current and recent research

### Multi-Agent Robotic Motion Lab

**Undergraduate Robotics Researcher · Jul 2025 – Jul 2026**

- Developed a sim-to-real pipeline using actuator networks for hierarchical reinforcement-learning-based loco-manipulation.
- Trained and deployed policies on the Unitree Go2 and HEBI Daisy Hexapod.
- Developed a reference-conditioned tracking policy for X-Morph to transfer human motion to quadruped, hexapod, and quadruped-with-arm morphologies.

### Evolution Innovation Lab (Raye Lab)

**Student Research Intern · Jul 2025 – Nov 2025**

- Designed a PID-enabled fluidic control board for soft robotic actuators with precise pressure regulation from 0–150 kPa.
- Integrated an Arduino GIGA microcontroller with a touchscreen interface and programmable pneumatic sequences.
- Authored an open technical guide with circuit schematics, CAD files, and Arduino source code.

### Electronic textiles

**Research Assistant, NUS Undergraduate Research · Aug 2024 – Dec 2024**

- Proposed and received a $5,000 grant for a project on wearable fabric electronics supervised by Professor Tural Khudiyev.
- Developed a soldering-based method for fabricating one-dimensional circuits with 96 interconnected micro-LEDs.
- Integrated the circuit with an Arduino microcontroller and evaluated it against thermally drawn fiber methods.
